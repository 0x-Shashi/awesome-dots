# Research and Briefings Dots

Autonomous agents and workflows configured for continuous market monitoring, competitor intelligence, and synthesis.

## Catalog

* [Daily Competitor Changelog Monitor](https://frankchiu.io/ai-chatgpt-dots/) - Monitored beat tracking competitor marketing releases, product updates, and patent filings while separating verified facts from speculative inferences.
* [arXiv Preprint Digest Agent](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots#work-with-your-dot) - Scans preprint repositories on a daily schedule, matches papers against team research keywords, and writes one-page summaries.
* [Regulatory and Compliance Tracker](https://dotsguide.com/what-are-dots) - Tracks industry regulatory body updates, flag policy changes, and prepares compliance briefs for legal teams.
* [InsurHOT Insurance Intelligence (alongor666/InsurHOT)](https://github.com/alongor666/InsurHOT) - Agent workflow collecting public insurance market disclosures and formatting them for structured data ingestion.
* [Executive Morning Digest Generator (therundown.ai)](https://app.therundown.ai/guides/how-to-use-chatgpt-dot) - Prepares a single source-backed news brief across designated financial and technology beats prior to business hours.
