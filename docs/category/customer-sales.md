# Customer and Sales Dots

Autonomous agents and workflows configured for sales prospecting, deal requirement synchronization, proposal drafting, and account follow-through.

## Catalog

* [Dynamic Sales Proposal Synchronizer](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots#work-with-your-dot) - Continuously connects client requirement notes to proposal decks, updating technical specifications as conversations evolve.
* [Inbound Lead Qualification Agent](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots#give-it-a-responsibility) - Categorizes incoming business inquiries, enriches company profiles from public data, and routes high-intent leads to account executives.
* [Customer Feedback Aggregator](https://flaviocopes.com/openai-dots/#give-it-a-responsibility-not-a-request) - Collects recurring customer support issues and feature requests, grouping them into prioritized product requirements.
* [Account Renewal Intelligence](https://dotsguide.com/pricing) - Monitors customer usage patterns and contractual timelines, preparing renewal briefs and flagged expansion opportunities for account teams.
* [CRM Contact Sync Assistant](https://github.com/openai/plugins) - Extracts new contacts and relationship updates from email threads and updates CRM customer records under human supervision.
