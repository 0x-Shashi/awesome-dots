# Finance and Operations Dots

Autonomous agents and workflows configured for expense tracking, subscription audits, invoice reconciliation, and operational oversight.

## Catalog

* [Recurring Subscription Auditor](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001530-getting-started-with-your-dot) - Scans accounting feeds and billing emails to identify redundant software seats, forgotten trials, and unannounced price hikes.
* [Invoice Reconciliation Agent](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots#work-with-your-dot) - Matches inbound vendor receipts against purchase orders, flags pricing discrepancies, and populates draft ledger entries.
* [Budget Threshold Guardian](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots/tasks-and-memory#recurring-tasks) - Periodically compares department cloud spend against monthly budgets, sending alerts only when projected burn exceeds limits.
* [Comparative Vendor Pricing Scout](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001529-dots-privacy-security-and-safety-faqs) - Gathers pricing quotes across multiple suppliers for enterprise equipment while maintaining strict user handoff for checkout.
* [Travel Expense Receipt Compiler](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots#give-it-a-responsibility) - Collects flight, hotel, and meal invoices from email, parses totals, and generates completed expense reports.
